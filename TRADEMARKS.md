# DevOS Trademark Guidelines

## Ownership

"DevOS", "Carai", "Carai Agency", "Caribbean Ai Agency" and associated logos and branding are trademarks or protected marks of their respective owners.

The DevOS source code license does not grant trademark rights.

## Permitted References

You may truthfully refer to DevOS when:

- identifying that your software uses DevOS;
- describing compatibility with DevOS;
- discussing DevOS;
- identifying a fork or derivative project, provided the reference does not imply official status.

For example:

- "Built with DevOS."
- "A community fork of DevOS."

## Prohibited Branding

Do not use DevOS or Carai branding in a manner that suggests:

- the project is an official Carai product;
- Carai endorses the project;
- the project is operated by Carai Agency;
- the project is an official DevOS distribution;
- Carai provides support or guarantees the project.

Forks should choose their own product name and visual identity.

Examples of names that should not be used without authorization include:

- Official Carai DevOS
- Carai DevOS Cloud
- Official DevOS Cloud
- Carai Agency DevOS
- any substantially similar branding that could reasonably imply official sponsorship or affiliation

## Commercial Products

Commercial products incorporating or interacting with DevOS must not use Carai branding or imply official affiliation without written permission.

The trademark policy is separate from the software license. A use permitted by the software license does not automatically grant permission to use Carai or DevOS trademarks.

For trademark questions or permission requests, contact Carai Agency.
